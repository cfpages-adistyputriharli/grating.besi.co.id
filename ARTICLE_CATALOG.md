# Article Catalog — grating.besi.co.id

Scope: 96 non-geographic article briefs that turn the steel-grating product subdomain into an evidence-led reference across fundamentals, selection, design inputs, procurement, fabrication, installation, maintenance, and end of life.

Domain boundaries: existing `/i-shape/`, `/plain-grating/`, and `/serrated-grating/` routes own commercial product intent; `/kontak/` owns current quotes. Articles do not promise engineering approval, universal performance, current stock, price, warranty, certification, or service coverage. Other owned domains may cover the same subject independently; anti-cannibalization applies only within `grating.besi.co.id`.

Catalog statistics: 16 parent topics; 96 briefs; six briefs per topic; zero geography-swapped briefs.

Priority legend: P0 establishes core route ownership and first-wave tools; P1 completes important selection and lifecycle paths; P2 deepens specialist coverage; P3 is lower-frequency reference content. Waves are bounded publication groups, not permission to publish everything at once.
