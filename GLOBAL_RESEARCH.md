## Cross-catalog fact bank

- Grating is an installed open-floor or access system: panel configuration, bearing direction, clear span, support, fixing, openings, edge details, use, exposure, and maintenance all matter.
- A nominal size, photo, mass, trade name, or one load-table value cannot prove the exact configuration or installed capacity.
- Plain, serrated, press-locked, welded, close-mesh, heavy-duty, expanded metal, wire mesh, plate, aluminium, stainless, and FRP are not interchangeable labels.
- Slip risk depends on surface, contamination, drainage, slope, footwear/wheels, cleaning, lighting, behavior, and the named test or assessment method.
- Open area does not by itself describe clear opening geometry, travel orientation, caster/heel risk, drainage behavior, or route accessibility.
- Corrosion and coating life depend on substrate, fabrication, preparation, process, geometry, exposure, drainage, contacts, damage, inspection, and compatible repair.
- Visible rust, movement, broken joints, missing clips, distortion, or blocked drainage are observations requiring safe control and competent diagnosis, not remote capacity verdicts.
- Foreign access, OSHA, and accessibility sources are technical decision aids; current Indonesian law, project rules, and authority approval govern locally.
